Asphalt Milling & Resurfacing

When an existing asphalt surface has deteriorated but the underlying pavement structure remains suitable, milling and resurfacing can provide an alternative to complete pavement replacement.

Asphalt milling removes the existing surface layer to prepare the pavement for a new asphalt overlay. This process can improve the appearance and ride quality of an existing paved area while addressing surface deterioration.

Whether milling and resurfacing is appropriate depends on the condition of the existing pavement, drainage, base, and surrounding site. We evaluate these factors before recommending the most suitable solution.

Our Asphalt Paving Process

A durable asphalt surface begins with proper planning and preparation. Our paving process focuses on evaluating site conditions, preparing a stable foundation, managing drainage, and installing quality asphalt so the finished pavement performs reliably for years.

Consultation & Site Evaluation

We discuss your paving goals, evaluate the existing property and pavement conditions, and identify site factors such as traffic, grading, drainage, and access requirements.

Site Preparation

Base Preparation

Finishing & Cleanup

Frequently Asked Questions

What asphalt paving services do you provide?

We provide new asphalt paving, residential driveway paving, commercial parking lot paving, asphalt repair and maintenance, milling and resurfacing, and related pavement improvements throughout Massachusetts.

Do you pave residential driveways?

Yes. We install and replace asphalt driveways for residential properties, including driveway areas that require grading, drainage improvements, additional parking, or related site preparation.

Do you pave commercial parking lots?

Yes. We provide asphalt paving and related site work for commercial parking areas and private commercial roads throughout Massachusetts.

How long does an asphalt driveway last?

The useful life of asphalt depends on installation quality, drainage, traffic, soil conditions, weather, and maintenance. Proper site preparation and ongoing maintenance can significantly improve long-term pavement performance.

Should I repair or replace my asphalt?

It depends on the extent and cause of the pavement damage. Localized problems may be repairable, while widespread deterioration, structural failure, or drainage problems may require resurfacing or replacement. We can evaluate the existing pavement and recommend the appropriate solution.

What is asphalt milling and resurfacing?

Milling removes a portion of the existing asphalt surface before a new asphalt layer is installed. It can be an effective way to restore suitable existing pavement without completely reconstructing the pavement system.

Does asphalt require maintenance?

Regular inspection and timely maintenance can help extend the useful life of asphalt pavement. The appropriate maintenance depends on the pavement's age, condition, traffic, drainage, and exposure to weather.

Can you address drainage problems during paving?

Yes. Drainage and grading are important parts of many paving projects. Depending on the property, improvements may include grading adjustments, drainage work, catch basins, or related site development.

How long does asphalt paving take?

Project duration depends on the size and complexity of the paving work, site preparation requirements, weather, access, and other site conditions. We can provide a project schedule after evaluating your property and project requirements.

Do you provide asphalt paving for private roads?

Yes. We install asphalt pavement for private roads and access areas, including projects that require excavation, grading, drainage, and other site preparation.

Do you serve both residential and commercial properties?

Yes. We provide asphalt paving and related services for residential homeowners, commercial properties, businesses, and other Massachusetts properties.

How much does asphalt paving cost in Massachusetts?

Asphalt paving costs vary depending on the size of the project, existing site conditions, excavation and base requirements, drainage, pavement thickness, access, and other project factors. We provide estimates based on the specific conditions and requirements of your property.
