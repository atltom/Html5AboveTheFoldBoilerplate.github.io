Transform Plain Foundations & Facades with Authentic Natural Stone

Exposed poured concrete foundations, dated brickwork, and plain siding detract from the architectural elegance of an otherwise beautiful home. S. Phaneuf & Associates installs authentic natural stone veneer throughout Massachusetts, turning bare structural walls into timeless New England stonework showpieces. From raised ranch foundation refacing in Groton to grand exterior chimney transformations in Wellesley and Lexington, our master masons deliver unmatched aesthetic distinction.

Unlike manufactured concrete faux stone (which fades, chips, and discolors over time), we specialize in 100% genuine quarried natural stone cut to thin veneer thickness (1 to 1.5 inches). You receive all the color depth, texture, and permanence of full-bed stone masonry without the need for expensive structural shelf angles or oversized concrete footings.

Moisture management is critical in New England. We install dual-layer weather-resistive barriers (WRB), heavy-gauge galvanized metal lath, and polymer-modified mortar scratch coats. This ensures water never penetrates behind the stone face and prevents freeze-thaw bond failure or efflorescence.

Moisture Protection & Masonry Adhesion Standards

Stone veneer longevity depends entirely on moisture prevention and bond strength. S. Phaneuf & Associates adheres to strict MVMA (Masonry Veneer Manufacturers Association) guidelines.

Dual-Layer Weather-Resistive Barrier (WRB)

Over wood-framed substrates, we install two layers of Grade D water-resistive barrier paper (or a continuous liquid-applied air/water barrier). This creates an internal drainage plane that prevents moisture from reaching the building framing.

Corrosion-Resistant Galvanized Metal Lath

We fasten 2.5 lb/yd² or 3.4 lb/yd² self-furring hot-dipped galvanized expanded diamond metal lath using corrosion-resistant fasteners driven into structural framing members, creating an unshakeable mechanical anchor.

Polymer-Modified Mortar Scratch Coat

We apply a 1/2-inch thick scratch coat of polymer-modified mortar (ANSI A118.4) and score it horizontally with a notched trowel. Once cured, this provides exceptional shear bond strength exceeding 300 PSI.

100% Back-Buttered Stone Application

Every stone is back-buttered with fresh bonding mortar before being firmly pressed into the scratch coat with a slight twisting motion. This achieves 100% mortar coverage with zero air pockets where freezing water could cause delamination.

Our 5-Step Stone Veneer Installation Process

Our meticulous installation workflow ensures architectural beauty and waterproof performance.

1. Surface Inspection & Substrate Prep

We inspect the wall surface, repair any substrate cracks, install flashings, and clean concrete or wood sheathing.

2. Moisture Barrier & Metal Lath Installation

We apply dual-layer water barriers and mechanically anchor heavy galvanized diamond lath with corrosion-resistant masonry fasteners.

3. Mortar Scratch Coat Application & Curing

Our masons apply the structural scratch coat, score horizontal keys, and allow proper cure time for maximum tensile strength.

4. Hand-Selecting, Dressing & Setting Stone

We lay out stones by color and cut, back-butter each unit with polymer mortar, set corner units first, and work inward with tight joints.

5. Joint Striking & Final Acid-Free Cleaning

We fill and tool joints with weather-resistant mortar (or dry-stack styling), brush clean the stone faces, and conduct a thorough quality inspection.

Frequently Asked Questions About Stone Veneer

What is the difference between natural thin stone veneer and manufactured faux stone?

Natural thin stone veneer is 100% genuine quarried earth (granite, fieldstone, limestone, quartzite) sawn to roughly 1 to 1.5 inches thick. It features authentic mineral color that will never fade, peel, or discolor from UV rays or salt. Manufactured faux stone is painted, molded concrete that can chip, fade over 10-15 years, and show gray core concrete if nicked. We exclusively install genuine natural stone veneer.

Can stone veneer be applied directly over an existing poured concrete foundation?

Yes. On clean, unpainted poured concrete foundations, we mechanically scarify the concrete surface, apply a bonding agent, and install a polymer-modified mortar scratch coat. If the concrete is painted or contaminated, we mechanically anchor galvanized expanded metal lath with masonry fasteners before applying the scratch coat to guarantee an unbreakable mechanical bond.

How does natural stone veneer hold up during Massachusetts winters?

When installed with correct weather-resistive barriers and high-performance polymer-modified mortar (ANSI A118.4 / A118.15), natural stone veneer is impervious to freeze-thaw cycles. Because natural quarried stone has extremely low water absorption, water cannot penetrate the stone matrix to cause spalling or freeze fracturing.

Can natural stone veneer be installed on home chimneys?

Yes. Refacing an outdated or crumbling brick chimney with natural New England fieldstone or ledgestone veneer is one of the most dramatic exterior architectural upgrades possible. We flash the roofline, repair underlying masonry, and apply stone veneer from foundation to chimney cap.

What stone styles and cuts are available?

We install mosaic (irregular fieldstone), square & rectangular, ashlar, and modern tight-fitting ledgestone cuts in a vast palette of New England granites, bluestones, and weathered fieldstones.
